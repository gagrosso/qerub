The problem

A leaked password shouldn't be enough to get in. Today it probably is.

Most Microsoft 365 tenants were set up once, in a hurry, and never reviewed again. MFA that isn't enforced for every user, external forwarding left open, no Conditional Access, third-party apps with full permissions, and admin accounts used for day-to-day work. Every one of those is a door left ajar.

An attacker with a leaked password gets in as if they were an employee: they read the email, study the invoices and, when the moment is right, impersonate a vendor or the CEO. Most BEC fraud starts exactly this way — with a mailbox compromised for weeks and nobody noticing.
