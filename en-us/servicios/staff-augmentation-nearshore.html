Q-Pod — Staff augmentation: senior nearshore teams

Senior talent embedded in your team in days, not months. Hiring a senior profile in AI, data, cloud or cybersecurity can take you six months — and plenty of agencies send you a junior in disguise. Q-Pod is different: senior teams that embed into yours, work in your time zone and are accountable for results, not seat-hours. No juniors billed as seniors.

The problem

You need seniority now, not in six months.

The senior IT talent market is dry and expensive. You post the role, wait months, and when someone good finally shows up, another company has already made them a counteroffer. Meanwhile, the project waits. And the "staffing" agencies fill the gap with profiles that aren't what they promised.

Q-Pod gives you access to a curated senior network, across three time zones, that joins in days and works as one more part of your team. You pay for results and for real seniority — not for a filled seat. And if a profile isn't the right fit, we swap them out at no cost.

Frequently asked questions

Before you start

What contract model does the team work under?

We agree on it based on your needs: by capacity (a senior profile embedded in your team, working off your backlog) or by results / fixed scope. We make it clear from the start who runs the day-to-day, how long it lasts, and the exit terms.

Are they really senior profiles only?

Yes, and it's verifiable: you see the resume, the years and the stack before you decide. We don't bill juniors at senior rates. And if the profile isn't the right fit within the first two weeks, we swap them out at no cost.

What's the advantage of nearshore from Argentina?

UTC-3 time zone: real overlap with Spain and with all of the Americas, including the U.S. East Coast — you iterate the same day. Strong English and savings versus hiring in-house, with equivalent quality. It's not "low cost," it's proximity and seniority.

Tell us which profile you're missing.

A 30-minute call. We present a senior shortlist in 5–10 days — with resume, years of experience and stack. No strings attached.
