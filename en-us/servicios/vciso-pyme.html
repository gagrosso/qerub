Security isn't a project that ends — it's a monthly discipline. We bring the judgment, the method and the evidence, without the cost of a security executive.

Term · Ongoing · monthly retainer Price · from €590/month NIST CSF 2.0 · NIS2 evidence
The problem

Everything got fixed in January. By October, nobody knows where anything stands.

The classic pattern: a company hardens its systems after a scare or an audit, and twelve months later it has all quietly drifted — new employees without MFA, connected apps no one reviewed, backups that silently stopped running. Security without follow-up has a shelf life.

Hiring a CISO runs into six figures and makes no sense for a 30-person company. What does make sense is buying the 8 monthly hours of judgment a CISO would spend on a company your size: reviewing, prioritizing, deciding and documenting all of it.

What's included

A monthly routine that keeps your security alive.

Monthly review

A check on the critical controls: MFA, access, backups, DMARC, alerts and new vulnerabilities.

Living risk register

An up-to-date risk register: what was accepted, what was mitigated, what's still open and why.

Mini executive committee

A 30-minute monthly meeting with leadership: 1 page, 3 decisions, zero jargon.

Annual incident drill

A response tabletop every year, with minutes and action items — the plan never goes rusty.

Evidence binder

Everything documented and dated: what NIS2, client audits and cyber insurance ask for.

Decision support

A direct line for questions: is this vendor safe? Is this email real? Should we sign this addendum?
