The problem

The cyber insurance questionnaire is an exam — and almost no one prepares for it.

To buy or renew cyber insurance, the insurer asks you to answer dozens of technical questions: MFA on every login? Immutable, tested backups? An incident response plan? Training? What you answer determines your premium, your coverage and the exclusions on your policy.

There are two costly mistakes: answering "yes" to something that isn't quite true (the insurer can deny the claim for a misrepresentation) or answering "no" to controls you could close in two weeks that would have lowered your premium. We get you to the honest middle ground: close what's closable and declare it with evidence.

Frequently asked questions

Before you sign up

Why am I being denied cyber insurance or charged more?

Insurers require minimum controls — MFA, tested backups, an incident response plan — and they raise the premium or exclude coverage if you don't have them. A poorly answered questionnaire can also void your coverage right when you need it most.

Do you sell insurance?

No. We're not a brokerage and we don't earn a commission from any insurer. We get you technically ready so you can buy on better terms from whomever you choose.

What if the assessment uncovers bigger problems?

We'll tell you plainly and without dramatizing. If needed, the Q-Start diagnosis sizes up the whole picture and sets priorities; cyber insurance comes afterward, on better terms.
