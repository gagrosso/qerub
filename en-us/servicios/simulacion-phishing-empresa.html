The problem

Someone on your team will click. The question is what happens next.

Social engineering is behind most incidents at small and midsize companies. And the lures have changed: perfectly written emails, with real context from your industry, generated with AI. The spam filter doesn't stop them all, and annual compliance training doesn't change anyone's behavior.

What does work, according to all the evidence in the field, is continuous training: short simulations every month, immediate feedback for whoever clicks, and metrics that leadership can track. The goal isn't to hunt down culprits — it's to build the reflex to distrust and report.
