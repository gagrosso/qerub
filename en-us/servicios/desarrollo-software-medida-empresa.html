The problem

Neither a "code factory" nor a legacy system that traps you.

Software factories sell you hours and juniors: lots of people, little judgment, and a result no one wants to maintain. At the other extreme, an old, tightly coupled system becomes the excuse to do nothing — and the biggest barrier to using AI or integrating anything new.

We bring in a few senior people who understand your business, build what's essential, and ship it to production, tested and documented. We modernize the legacy in pieces, without stopping operations, so that afterward you can connect data, AI, or whatever comes next. And from day one, ownership and the clean exit are written in.

Frequently asked questions

Before we start

Are you a "code factory"?

No. We don't bill junior hours against a pile of tickets. We work with senior judgment end to end: discovery, design, development, security, and go-live. A few good people, not many cheap ones.

Who owns the code and the data?

Yours, always. We hand over the repository, documentation, and a full handoff. There's no lock-in: if one day you want to continue on your own or with another vendor, you can. The clean exit is written in from the start.

Tell us what you need to build.

A 30-minute technical conversation with the people who'll do the work. We leave with a clear scope and a fixed price — and if now isn't the right time, we'll tell you.
