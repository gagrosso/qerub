The problem

The pilot that never reaches production.

There's excitement about AI, someone builds a proof of concept with ChatGPT or a data notebook, it works in the demo… and that's where it dies. It doesn't scale, no one maintains it, and the business decision is still made by gut feeling. The bottleneck is almost never the model: it's data that's scattered, low-quality and ungoverned.

That's why we don't separate AI from data: they're the same force. We organize the data, choose the use case that truly moves the needle, and measure against an agreed baseline. If it doesn't improve, it doesn't move forward. Whatever reaches production stays monitored, documented and under your control.

Frequently asked questions

Before we start

Is this for me if I don't have a data team?

Yes. It's designed precisely for companies without data scientists on staff: we bring the senior judgment, leave it running in production and train your people to maintain it. If your leadership team doesn't understand it, we haven't done our job right.

How do you keep the pilot from stalling before production?

We start with the data and a use case with measurable ROI, validate against a baseline and only scale if it improves. And we deploy with monitoring and handover, not a PDF. It ties directly to the security of AI usage so everything stays governed.

A technical conversation, no sales deck.

Thirty minutes with the people who actually do the work. We'll tell you where we'd start with your data — and if it's not the right time yet, we'll tell you that too.
