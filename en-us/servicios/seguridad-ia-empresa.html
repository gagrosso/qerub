The problem

"Shadow AI" is the new data leak.

Contracts, payroll, patient or client data end up in free AI tools through personal accounts, beyond any company oversight. Every paste is a potential data transfer that GDPR requires you to control — and that no antivirus will ever catch.

The other extreme costs money too: banning everything. The team loses the productivity AI offers, or uses it anyway from their phones. The way out isn't to ban or ignore: it's to define which tools, with which accounts and which data — and to properly configure the ones you already have, starting with Copilot.

Frequently asked questions

Before you hire us

Why do I need an AI policy if we already ban ChatGPT?

Banning doesn't work: your team uses it anyway from their phones, with no oversight. A realistic policy defines what can be used, with which data and which accounts — turning hidden risk into productive, supervised use.

What's special about Microsoft 365 Copilot?

Copilot respects your existing permissions — and that's the problem: if your internal permissions have been wide open for years, Copilot will hand anyone documents they shouldn't see. That's why it pairs so well with our Microsoft 365 security review.

Is this against using AI?

Quite the opposite. We run on AI ourselves. The goal is for your team to use it more and better — with clear rules about client data, corporate accounts and approved tools.
